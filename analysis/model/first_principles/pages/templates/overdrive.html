Overdrive

The same ring, driven on past its dead end and kept going — a full 360° of the fold parameter. The physical range is the 60° arc from −60° to 0°. Beyond it the bodies pass through each other and through their own centres, the spacing falls to zero and goes negative, and the plates turn inside out and back. Fronts are painted solid, backs pale and dashed. Joints are held by identity and checked on every frame.
