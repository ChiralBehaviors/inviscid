Patch of Record

5×5×5 cells, voids empty. Positions are the model's own output, frame by frame. Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom.

What is being drawn

The lattice. Sites are the integer points of a cubic grid. Only the all-even sites hold a cell; the all-odd sites are the octahedral voids and hold nothing. This patch is the 125 even sites in −4…4, i.e. a 5×5×5 block of cells. Each cell is centred at L(a)·site, where L(a) is the lattice constant of the shared-face law, stationary at a = −30°.

One cell. 12 vertices, 24 rigid struts of length √2, 8 rigid triangular plates, and one fold angle a: a = 0 is the vector equilibrium, a = −60 the octahedron. Between them the eight plates rotate about their own normals and move inward, Fuller's jitterbug.

The joints. A cell touches each of its six axis neighbours (±2 along x, y or z) at two vertices across the square opening. Those two vertex pairs, read at a = −30 and held, are the joints — the orange dots. The eight diagonal neighbours at (±1,±1,±1) are voids, so there is no triangular-face joint anywhere. At a = 0 two more vertices of the same pair of cells touch momentarily (the square closes); at a = −60 the two joints merge into one point.

Breathe. Every cell at the same fold angle, a running 0 → −60 → 0, centres riding L(a). Nothing else moves. This is the medium's one free motion: every joint stays exactly closed throughout.

Kick. Soft joints: each joint is a spring V = ½ k |Δ|² on the separation of its two tied vertices, k = 1; masses are corner point masses. At t = 0 the centre cell alone is given fold rate 0.2 rad per unit time and the block evolves freely (midpoint integration, step 0.005). Colour is each cell's fold-angle deviation from −30°. The Show menu separates the two things a cell can do: fold only pins every cell's centre and orientation and draws just its fold angle, so a fold wave appears as shape; rigid only freezes every fold at −30° and draws the cells' translation and rotation. The energy line reports how the block's energy is divided among those motions and the joint springs. The motion is small. At this kick the kicked cell's vertices move at most 12% of a strut and the surface cells about 3%; full motion and fold only agree to 0.2% of a strut, cell centres travel under 0.3% of a strut and cells rotate under 0.1° over the whole run, so the colour is the reliable signal and fold ×5 exaggerates the fold deviation fivefold to make the wave visible as shape. In these units the two sound speeds are 1/(2√2) and 1/2 lattice constants per unit time, so the front reaches the block's faces around t ≈ 4–6 and reflects.

Kick, network. The same block and the same kick with the cell constraint removed. Each of the 1000 triangles is a free rigid body (6 coordinates, unit mass lumped to its corners); every junction is one rubber blob tying all the corners that meet there — four in the bulk, two where a jitterbug's own hinge has no partner at the surface — with V = ½k Σ|xcorner − junction mean|², k = 1. No shared fold, no rigid hinge. The centre cell's eight triangles start with the velocities the fold-rate kick gives them; the network evolves freely (velocity Verlet, step 0.01). Colour is each triangle's rotation about its own normal in the fold's sign convention, so it is comparable to the Kick tab. The energy line splits kinetic energy into spin about the normal, translation, and tilt.
