The model of record · in physics terms

Four statements

1

Exactly one free motion

Every cell breathing together. On any closed block one internal motion costs nothing; every other motion stretches a joint. Seven zero modes — six rigid plus the breathe — on every block from 8 to 216 cells, identified by vector.

2

Two sound speeds, ratio √2

A real dispersion relation: 7 branches, 4 zero at Γ. Speeds 1/(2√2) and 1/2, each doubly degenerate; optical ω² = 12/5. Every frequency ∝ √(joint stiffness), which is unmeasured on the rig — so the ratios and closed forms are the result.

3

The front obeys the joint law alone

Joint force ~ separationn−1 gives front speed ~ A(n−2)/n. n = 2: amplitude-independent to 0.000% over 8×. n = 5/2 (Hertz): 0.1993 vs 1/5 — the project's one external check. Unchanged by emptying the voids.

4

A finite block rings; its lowest note is sound

Spectrum tops out below the band ceiling, 1.463 → 1.694 over sides 2–6 toward √3; the bottom falls as size−0.95. No gap above the one free motion.

The honeycomb · 15 bodies · one internal degree of freedom

The medium's one motion

A compact patch of the honeycomb: seven cells (copper) and eight voids (verdigris), welded plate to plate. The two families exchange — at a = 0° the cells sit at the VE and the voids are closed octahedra; at a = −60° they have completely swapped. The lattice constant breathes 1 → 2/√3 → 1 on the way. Nothing tumbles, and nothing passes through itself: |fold| = 60° is where a body folds through its own octahedron, a hard stop rather than a convention. Void fold = cell fold + 60° forces both families into that window at once, which is why the range is exactly sixty degrees.

cells a = −30.0° · voids b = +30.0° · L = 1.1547

The swing is dynamics, not a sweep: with one degree of freedom the angle obeys ä = −½(M′/M)ȧ², and the patch's effective mass runs 272 → 120 → 264 across the window — fastest at mid-swing, lingering where the cells are most open, bouncing elastically off both stops. Every weld here closes to 9·10⁻¹⁶ across the whole window; scale the lattice law by 1% and they tear open — the geometry is that tight. The live version is HoneycombBreatheAnimation.

The tied array, from first principles

Built one body at a time, each step measured and shown. Owner: “a truly interesting result.”

  1. One cell passes the VE with its twelve joints intact. The VE is where it chooses its sense: the two octahedra it can close to merge the joints on the six points in different pairings.
  2. Tied as they are, an octahedron opens one way only — the mirror opening violates the joints at first order, even as a free linkage.
  3. An octahedron hung on a VE face is a passenger: one shared plate ties nothing but the plate. Hanging more changes nothing.
  4. A void bounded by plates of different VEs in a closed ring is a VE that expands: the ring has one motion, void fold = cell fold + 60°, and no cell rotates.
  5. So the tied array's motion is one sixty-degree segment with an octahedron dead-end at each end. A cell and its void are one jitterbug's motion split in two.
  6. At a vertex point of the VE state four cells meet — eight corners on one point, two permanent joints. They stop the block passing the VE by meeting head-on. Which pairs are tied is the array's chirality, chosen when it was tied.
  7. The overdrive: continue the parametrisation through 360°. Every weld holds by identity; plates turn inside out in two waves; at +60° and +240° the whole array is one coincident octahedron, then re-expands mirrored.

The holonomy campaign

The question: the fold circle closes in configuration space but each plate's orientation traverses a full turn — a classical Z2 holonomy. Is it real, and does anything physical ride on it? Eight self-gating modules answered it, every row a measured PASS/FAIL with its exit code as the verdict.

GateModuleWhat it measured
G0lift.py The instrument: a sign-continuous SU(2) lift. Trivial loop +q, 2π loop −q, 4π loop +q; refuses coarse and unclosed paths.
G1plate_holonomy.py One circuit of the fold circle is −q for 432 of 432 plates — ring 32, hc15 120, block 280, cells and voids alike. The plate-orientation law holds to 2·10⁻¹⁵ through the whole cycle.
G2joint_twist.py Relative lift across both permanent vertex joints is +q for all 8 cross pairs, despite 180° excursions. No Z2 survives in any pairwise relative history — the sign exists only against an external frame. (Subsumed G3.)
G4inside_out_cover.py X(a+180°) = −X(a) exact, even uncentred. Sheet-touching is patch geometry, not physics: one body is centrosymmetric at every fold; hc15's sheets are ambiently coincident at every angle.
G5doubled_block.py The §3c double, built: every half-weld closed onto the twin sheet, residual 9·10⁻¹⁶. spec(double) = spec(free) ⊎ spec(Dirichlet) exactly. Beats free ~3×; torus-level from side 3.
G6screw_sector.py Negative — a result, not a failure. No screw sector on the medium: 0/48 isometries pair cell and void bodies at generic folds. Glide pairing exists only at a = −30°, and belongs to the retired covering.
G7physical_arc.py The physical arc never exercises the holonomy: crossing-free on [−60°, 0°], walls within 1° beyond both ends; breathe of any amplitude returns +q. Only monotone traversal of the full circle reaches −q.
τscrew_prerequisites.py τ⁶ is a pure translation, exactly; a single τ leaves the coherent family (parity demands +60° and −60° at once). Free and properly discontinuous on the arc only. Bieberbach naming stays withdrawn.

Owner decision 22 · 2026-09-01 · “double for sure. that is the most elegant”

The closure of record is the double

Finite patches need a boundary story. Three candidates were measured: free boundaries, the periodic torus, and the doubled block — a second copy glued along the boundary plate-backs, closing every half-weld with real constraints on a real assembly. No wrapped-index operator surgery, no rig. The double realises the branched picture of note §3c as a buildable constraint system, and that constructibility is the elegance the owner named.

Free runs ≈3× the double's distance at every size tried (sides 2–5) — and loses at matched cell count too: double(4), 128 cells, .0607 against free(5), 125 cells, .1452. The accepted cost: the double is 2× the cells of the box it closes.

spec(double) = spec(free) ⊎ spec(Dirichlet), exactly. The Neumann sector keeps all seven zero modes — six rigid plus the breathe — where the torus keeps only four. The wrapped torus operator matches the Bloch bands at commensurate k and stays the exact reference when a measurement wants it.

What it binds: future finite-patch work on the jitterbug medium — spectra, wave propagation, closed-patch experiments, in either language — defaults to doubled_block.double(side). It does not bind the automaton line; adoption in any particular visualization is per-work, the owner's call.
