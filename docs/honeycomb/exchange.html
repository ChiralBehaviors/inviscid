The cells change places, and the lattice returns

A cuboctahedron cell and the octahedral cell filling the hole beside it, sharing one triangular face. No overlap at any point.

At a = 0 the two cells meet on the shared face's plane — a cuboctahedron and an octahedron, the packing Fuller's honeycomb is made of. Drive the phase down and the constraint holds to 1e-16 while the cells trade shape: the VE closes toward the octahedron as the hole cell opens toward the VE. This is a valid packing throughout, and it returns to itself with the roles swapped.
